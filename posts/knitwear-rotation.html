Knitwear That Earns Its Place in Rotation

SeasonalJuly 2026

Not all knitwear is created equal, and rotation is where the difference shows. A good jumper gets better with wear; a cheap one pills by October and is gone by January.

Five silhouettes worth the investment this season: the V-neck vest layered over a shirt, the relaxed crewneck in a heavier gauge, the turtleneck in a fine merino, the cropped cardigan, and — controversially — one bold cable knit, worn once a week, never more.

What to let go of

Anything acrylic-heavy, anything that's already pilling, and anything you're keeping "just in case." Knitwear rewards a small, considered collection over a drawer full of options you never reach for.
